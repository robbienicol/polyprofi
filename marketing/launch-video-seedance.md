# Pathey — 60s Launch Video

**Seedance settings:** 1080p, 24fps, 9:16 for social / 16:9 for the App Store preview. Generate each shot separately at the stated duration.

**Consistency:** paste the STYLE BLOCK at the end of every Seedance prompt, and reuse the same seed across all of them. For continuity between consecutive Seedance shots, feed the last frame of the previous clip in as the image-to-video input.

**STYLE BLOCK** (append verbatim to every Seedance prompt):

```
Cinematic, shallow depth of field, near-black background (#141312), single warm terracotta light source (#D9653D) as the only saturated colour, cool desaturated shadows, subtle film grain, anamorphic, 24fps, slow deliberate camera. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no user interface elements.
```

Seedance cannot render legible text or UI. Every screen in this script is a real capture from the app.

## Copy rules

Nothing in the video may state or imply a return, a win rate, or a profit figure. No "make money", no "beat the market", no dollar outcome shown as a result. The goal amount on screen is a *target the user typed*, never a result — if a screen capture shows a filled progress ring, keep it partial and unlabelled. It's a finance app: implied performance claims are the fastest route to a rejected App Store review and an FCA problem.

## Shot budget

6 Seedance clips (30s of generation) and 4 screen captures. Generate each Seedance shot 3–4 times and pick — shots 1 and 4 are the ones most likely to need re-rolls, since wide landscapes drift.
